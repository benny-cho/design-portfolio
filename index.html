About Me
I’m From Worcester, Massachusetts

Say it with me, “Wuss-ter!”

I live here with my adoring wife, our two wonderful children, and one stubborn cat.

When I’m not working, you can find me at a baseball game, riding motorcycles, painting in my makeshift studio, sipping a cold brew even in the winter, or hustling in the gym.

My Past Experiences

My formal degree is in Audio & Media Technology. I started out working in recording studios and live audio production (I know what all of those knobs do!). Through a series of pivots, I found myself working in graphic design & web development.

I find immense joy from building things, solving problems, and creating good vibes.

What I’m Doing Now

Today, I’m the Creative Manager at MembersFirst, a digital agency & software provider for the private club industry. I lead a team of designers & front-end developers to create custom bespoke websites. Under my direction as a working manager, our Design Team produces award-winning websites for industry-leading clientele.
